## Using VibePress for your own site
You're welcome to install, theme, and extend VibePress for your own project. A small "Powered by VibePress" link appears in the
footer by default; you can turn it off in **Admin > Theme**. If you redistribute VibePress itself (not just a site built with it),
please keep this file and the credit in `README.md` intact — that's the easiest way to give credit if you're short on time to write
your own.
